# Henk Robbers (creator of AHCC) - in memoriam

Henk Robbers, the author of **AHCC** (this compiler) and of much of the Atari ST's
open-source tooling and desktop software, has **passed away**. He was **80** years
old. This file records what the community knows, with sources, so future readers of
this mirror understand who built the tool they are using.

## Life and work
Henk wrote an open-source C compiler for the Atari ST, based on Sozobon C and made
compatible with Pure C so existing libraries could be reused. In a 2003 interview he
described the project that became AHCC:

> "I'm writing an open source C compiler for Atari. It's based upon Sozobon C and will
> be compatible with Pure C. So it will be possible to use existing libraries. ...
> My compiler won't be as good or fast as Pure C, but you will be able to use it on an
> ST." - Henk Robbers, MyAtari interview, September 2003
> (mirror: https://www.exxosforum.co.uk/atari/mirror/myatari/issues/sep2003/henk.htm )

His other well-known Atari ST projects include **XaAES** (a GEM AES replacement),
**TeraDesk** (a desktop/shell), and **TT-Digger**.

## Final years and passing
- His personal homepage `members.chello.nl/h.robbers` is no longer online.
- His last known contact with the community was an email in **November 2024**.
- His death was reported on the Polish Atari forum (Atarionline.pl / Atarum.pl) on
  **4 December 2025**, at the age of **80**:
  https://atarionline.pl/forum/comments.php?DiscussionID=5119&page=3

## Bequest to the Atari Museum (Netherlands)
Following his death, his family member **Adinda Robbers** donated Henk's large
collection of Atari ST books, manuals, magazines and software to the Atari Museum in
the Netherlands, announced on the museum's "What's new?" page on **16 February 2026**:

> "New additions to the collection: A nice large lot of Atari ST books, manuals,
> magazines and software. Bequeathed by Henk Robbers (creator of XaAES, TeraDesk, and
> AHCC) and donated by Adinda Robbers. Thank you Adinda. Also thank you to Anand
> Sewrajsing for bringing these items to the museum."
> (https://atarimuseum.nl/whats-new/ )

## Maintained today by the community
With the original author gone, AHCC and his other projects survive only through
community mirrors. This mirror tracks the distribution of **AHCC 6.0** (27 Nov 2020).
The active upstream mirror is Brian Swetland's GitHub mirror:
https://github.com/swetland/ahcc
(see `README.md` in this directory for the mirror provenance, and the H3d fix note
in `h3d_fix/` for this project's one-line compiler bug fix + the fork used to submit it).
